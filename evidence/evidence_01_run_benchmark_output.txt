Reading the table:
  * Diagnosis pays for itself: without it the system still repairs most faults,
    but needs 22 attempts instead of 14 - it is guessing rather than choosing.
  * Verification costs nothing when diagnosis is good (+0 tasks) but -2 tasks
    when diagnosis is poor. The two are not independent: the verifier earns its
    keep exactly when the rest of the chain is weak.
  * Forbidding irreversible operators loses the one fault that genuinely needs
    an environment-level repair. That is the safety/capability trade-off, priced.
